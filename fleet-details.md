### Formatting notes for your site
- Your existing fleet cards use the pattern: **Name → Seat count → Category → one-line blurb**, matching what's above.
- Turboprops (ATR 72, Q400) also technically belong to "Turbo Prop," but since your list placed them under commercial airliner types, they're kept there — happy to move them if you'd rather group by propulsion type instead of use-case.
- All range/speed figures are the manufacturer's best-case (max fuel, no wind, standard payload) — real-world sector range with pax + baggage will be shorter, especially in Himalayan high-altitude/hot conditions. Worth a caveat line on the site if you're quoting these numbers publicly.
